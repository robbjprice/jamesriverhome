Outdoor living

Step outside and the property opens up.

The large covered front deck is built for slow mornings, evening meals, and quiet weather-watching, with a natural gas BBQ hookup ready for outdoor cooking.

Behind the home, a private fire-pit area sits within the forest, complemented by two custom-built woodsheds and the kind of outdoor space that feels considered rather than staged.
